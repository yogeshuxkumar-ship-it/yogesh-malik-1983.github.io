The problem

Employees may need time away, a workplace adjustment, or both. The experience crosses HR, a people manager, payroll, timekeeping and policy teams. Internal handoffs can feel like a maze to the employee.

  • People repeat information or cannot tell what happens next.
  • Case owners track deadlines across inboxes, spreadsheets and systems.
  • Managers plan coverage without needing sensitive health details.
  • Leave, accommodation and return events are related but fragmented.

UX challenge

How might we make the next action obvious, preserve a supportive conversation and keep records dependable—without exposing more information than a role needs?

ClarityContinuityPrivacyHuman reviewTraceability
Design tension: standardize enough to prevent missed steps while leaving room for individual circumstances and qualified judgment.

Four role journeys

Employee

Describe support needed, send requested items, track status, respond and plan return.

Leave specialist

Review information, validate policy with human judgment, communicate and own due dates.

People manager

Plan coverage and coordinate agreed work changes using limited operational details.

Program admin

Monitor queues, audit workflow consistency, reconcile integrations and refine templates.
